# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html)
(version 2.1). Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Harassment,
hateful conduct, or bad-faith disruption will lead to removal from the project.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers via the SECURITY.md contact
below. Reports are reviewed confidentially; the maintainers' decision on
enforcement is final for this project.
